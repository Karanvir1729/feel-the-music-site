iPhone + Mac

Music you can feel.

A Mac plays the music. Every iPhone on the same Wi‑Fi plays it in sync and vibrates with the beat.

iPhone app

Feel the Music

Finds the Mac on your Wi‑Fi, plays the stream in time with every other phone in the room, and turns the beat into haptics you feel in your hand. A demo mode plays a built‑in track with haptics, no Mac needed.

Mac app

Feel the Music Conductor

Captures whatever your Mac is playing, keeps the Mac's own speakers muted, and streams the audio to the phones over your local network.

How it works

  1. Play music on the Mac. Open Feel the Music Conductor and play anything. The Conductor captures the Mac's audio and keeps the Mac itself silent while it streams.
  2. Phones join over Wi‑Fi. Open Feel the Music on each iPhone connected to the same Wi‑Fi network. The phones find the Mac on their own. There is nothing to pair and no account to create.
  3. Everyone plays in sync and feels the beat. Every phone plays the same audio at the same moment and turns the rhythm into haptics.

No Mac nearby? The iPhone app's demo mode plays a built‑in track with haptics, so you can try it on its own.

Private by design

There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers. Audio and timing messages travel only between your Mac and your phones on your local network. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Read the privacy policy.
