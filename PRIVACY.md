    # Privacy Policy

    Effective September 15, 2026 · Last updated September 15, 2026

    This policy covers both apps made by Karanvir Khanna ("we"):

    
      - Feel the Music for iPhone (com.karanvirkhanna.feelthemusic)

      - Feel the Music Conductor for Mac (com.karanvirkhanna.feelthemusic.conductor)

    

    
      
## The short version

      
        - We do not collect any data. Not personal data, not usage data, not crash reports.

        - There are no accounts, no sign‑in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third‑party SDKs.

        - We run no servers. The apps never contact the internet. Everything travels only between your Mac and your phones on your local network.

      
    

    
## What the apps send over your local network

    The whole point of Feel the Music is to move sound from a Mac to nearby iPhones, so the two apps do talk to each other. This is what they exchange, and only ever on the local network you are connected to:

    
      - Audio. The Conductor captures what your Mac is playing, encodes it and streams it to the phones.

      - Timing and beat messages. Clock‑sync messages that keep the phones in step, and the haptic events the phones turn into vibrations.

      - A phone status report. When a phone joins, it tells the Mac its device model and name as reported by iOS, its iOS version, its current audio output (speaker or headphones) and a few timing statistics. While connected it keeps sending timing statistics and its thermal state, so the Mac can list the phone on its dashboard and keep it in sync.

    
    All of this goes directly between your Mac and your phones. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and none of it leaves your network.

    
## What is stored, and where

    
      - On the Mac, the Conductor writes a diagnostic log of each session (timing statistics and the phone status reports described above) to a folder named feel-the-music-runs in your home folder. It stays on your Mac and you can delete it whenever you like. The Conductor does not keep a copy of the audio it streams.

      - On the iPhone, the app keeps no recordings and no personal data.

      - With us, nothing. We have no way to see anything the apps do.

    

    
## Permissions and why we ask for them

    
      iPhone · Feel the Music
      
### Local Network

      Needed to find the Mac on your Wi‑Fi (the Conductor announces itself with Bonjour) and to receive the audio and timing stream from it. If you decline, the phone cannot find the Mac; the demo mode still works because it plays a built‑in track and does not use the network.

    
    
      iPhone · Feel the Music
      
### Bluetooth

      You may see a Bluetooth entry in the app's permission descriptions. Version 1.0 does not use Bluetooth and does not ask for Bluetooth access. The phone connects to the Mac over Wi‑Fi only.

    
    
      Mac · Feel the Music Conductor
      
### System Audio Recording

      Needed to capture what your Mac is playing so it can be streamed to the phones. macOS shows this request under Screen & System Audio Recording in System Settings. The Conductor captures audio only; it never records or looks at your screen. The audio is streamed to your phones and is not saved.

    
    
      Mac · Feel the Music Conductor
      
### Local Network

      Needed to announce the Conductor to phones on your Wi‑Fi and to stream to them.

    
    
      Mac · Feel the Music Conductor
      
### Bluetooth and Microphone

      Both appear in the app's permission descriptions but neither is used in normal operation, and the app does not ask for them. They belong to a developer measurement mode that only runs when the app is launched with a developer flag. Even in that mode, nothing leaves your Mac or your local network.

    

    
## Children

    The apps are not directed at children under 13, and they collect no information from anyone of any age.

    
## Your choices

    
      - On iPhone, you can turn Local Network access on or off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

      - On Mac, you can revoke System Audio Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.

      - You can delete the feel-the-music-runs folder on your Mac at any time.

      - Deleting an app removes everything it kept on that device.

    

    
## Changes to this policy

    If the apps ever start doing something different with your data, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version ships.
