Support

Help for Feel the Music on iPhone and Feel the Music Conductor on Mac. If the answer is not here, email prokaranvir@gmail.com.

Requirements
  • An iPhone running iOS 17 or later with Feel the Music installed.
  • A Mac running macOS 14.4 or later with Feel the Music Conductor installed.
  • Both on the same Wi‑Fi network.
Quick start
  1. Open Feel the Music Conductor on the Mac and allow System Audio Recording and Local Network when asked.
  2. Open Feel the Music on each iPhone and allow Local Network when asked.
  3. Play music on the Mac. The phones play it in sync and vibrate with the beat.

Frequently asked questions

My phone can't find the Mac.

  • Make sure the iPhone and the Mac are on the same Wi‑Fi network. A phone on cellular data or on a different network will not see the Mac.
  • Allow Local Network access for the app. If you tapped "Don't Allow" when the app first asked, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Feel the Music.
  • Make sure Feel the Music Conductor is open on the Mac and has Local Network access (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network).
  • Some guest, hotel, office and campus networks stop devices from seeing each other. A home network or a router you control works best.
  • Still nothing? Quit and reopen the app on the phone so it searches again.

There's no sound coming from the Mac.

That is expected. While the Conductor is streaming, the Mac's own speakers are kept muted so the sound comes from the phones and everyone stays in sync. The Mac's sound comes back when you stop streaming or quit the Conductor.

The phone isn't vibrating.

Haptics only play while the phone is unlocked with Feel the Music open on screen. If the phone locks or you switch to another app, the vibrations stop until you come back.

The audio through my AirPods or Bluetooth speaker is late.

Bluetooth adds its own delay on top of the stream, so AirPods, Bluetooth headphones and Bluetooth speakers will sound late compared with the other phones and with the haptics. For the tightest sync, play through the phone's built‑in speaker.

Can I try it without a Mac?

Yes. The iPhone app's demo mode plays a built‑in track with haptics on the phone alone. It does not need a Mac or a network connection.

Does either app collect anything?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers; the apps only talk to each other on your local network. See the privacy policy.
